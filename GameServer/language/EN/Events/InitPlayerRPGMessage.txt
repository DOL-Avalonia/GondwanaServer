ServerRPGMessage.ServerCommandsHeader:	--------- NEW SERVER COMMANDS ---------
ServerRPGMessage.CmdHelp:				/cmdhelp: Display the list of all commands available in the game.
ServerRPGMessage.CmdLanguage:			/language set <EN | FR | DE | ES | IT | NL | CS | RO | VI>: Set your account language so all texts, descriptions, and information will be translated.
ServerRPGMessage.CmdAutotranslate:		/autotranslate <on|off> (or /at): Turn on the autotranslator to automatically translate other players' dialogs, quests, and books into your language.
ServerRPGMessage.CmdMarket:				/market <open|close|name>: Deploy, rename, or close your personal market.
ServerRPGMessage.CmdCraft:				/craftmacro <set|clear|show|buy|buyto>: Automate your crafting queue and easily buy missing recipe ingredients directly from merchants.
ServerRPGMessage.CmdCombineList:		/combine list: Display your successfully discovered alternative craft item combinations.
ServerRPGMessage.CmdEvent:				/event: Display informations regarding current world events.
ServerRPGMessage.CmdEpicMount:			/epicmount: Epic flying mount controls - Usage: /epicmount [cruise | move | target | left | right | stop | rise | down | land].
ServerRPGMessage.CmdVol:				/vol or /steal <player>: Steal money or precious Territory Relics from rival players! It also allows you to pilfer treasures looted from an opposing clan's chests in PvP.
ServerRPGMessage.CmdFaceMob:			/facemob <mobname>: Turn towards the direction where the specified mob is located in your region. Very useful for quests!
ServerRPGMessage.CmdAskName:			/askname: Ask for your target's name in order to display it. You must do this before you can add them as a friend.
ServerRPGMessage.CmdGenistar:			/genistar <care|rename|emote|visibleweapon|addweapon|removeweapon>: Interact with and customize your Genistar pet. Target your egg to care for it, grant your grown companion a custom name, direct its emotes, and arm it for battle by managing its visible combat gear.
ServerRPGMessage.CmdPvP:				/pvp info & /pvp scores: Display information and current scores for the active PvP session.
ServerRPGMessage.CmdRvR:				/rvr info: Display information and current scores for the active RvR campaign.
ServerRPGMessage.CmdBet:				/bet <Team1 or Team2> <amount>: Place bets on fighters within active Arena rounds (Team1 vs Team2), up to {0} Gold coins. Check active teams and pools with '/bet list'.
